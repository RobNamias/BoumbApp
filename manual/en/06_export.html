💾 Export & Recording

← Back to Index

1. Live Recording

Capture your live performance instantly.

  • Click the Record Button (Red Circle) in the Transport Bar.
  • Perform your track (mute tracks, play synths, etc.).
  • Click Stop when finished.
  • Enter a name and download the .webm audio file.

2. Offline Export (WAV)

Render your full song arrangement properly.

  • Arrange your song in Skyline Mode.
  • Go to Project Menu > Export WAV.
  • Choose a filename.
  • The engine will render your song faster than real-time.
  • Download the high-quality .wav file (16-bit PCM).
